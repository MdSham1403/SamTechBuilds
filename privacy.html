Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form we receive your name, email, business name (optional), the service you need, budget range (optional) and your project description.

How it is used

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and discuss your project. Enquiries are received through Netlify Forms and as a private notification to the studio. We do not sell your details.

Third parties

  • Netlify hosts this website and processes form submissions.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, which means your browser contacts Google when the page loads.
  • If you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply.

Your choices

To ask us to correct or delete an enquiry you sent, email samtechbuilds@gmail.com.
